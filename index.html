Out for Career, an alumni career network

LiveFounding Product Lead · volunteer · 2026 to present

A private community where alumni who left the Haredi world help each other into careers in tech. A needs survey of 19 alumni put lectures first (16 of 19) and mentoring last (3 of 19), so I recommended dropping the mentoring bot that had been planned.

How I work

I write the spec and the acceptance criteria, coding agents build it, and I verify the result. When a decision matters, the model extracts and code decides.

  • Listen first. A survey, a recorded meeting, a 27-question brief. The plan changes when the answers say so.
  • Positioning before design. Name the real competitor, which is usually a belief, not a product.
  • Metrics set up front. The KPI, the decision rule and the number that must stay at zero, before any code.
  • Agents as a team. Plan, build, review in turns, with privacy, consent and accessibility checked along the way.

Most of this work holds client or member data, so the repositories are private. I'm glad to walk through the specs and decisions in conversation.
